# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Clients** browsing and comparing advisors (financial, business, career, or similar consulting expertise) and booking a session. They often arrive at a consequential decision point — money, career, a business call — so trust and clarity matter more than persuasion. Primary job: find a credible advisor fast and book with zero friction.

**Advisors** managing their practice: incoming bookings, client chat, profile and application/KYC status. Primary job: know at a glance where every booking, message, and application stands, without digging.

**Admins** (secondary, lower priority for now) reviewing advisor applications and moderating the marketplace. Job: triage dense status lists quickly and correctly.

## Product Purpose

AdvisorConnect is a two-sided marketplace connecting clients with vetted advisors for paid consulting sessions — discovery, booking, in-app chat, and practice/application management, end to end. Success looks like a client going from landing to a confirmed booking with an advisor they trust, and an advisor running their whole practice (bookings, chat, status) from one dashboard without leaving the product.

## Brand Personality

**Grounded, exacting, quietly confident.** This is not a bank app and not a hustle-culture growth tool — it should feel like sitting across from an advisor who has done this a thousand times and isn't trying to sell you anything. Confidence comes from restraint and precision, not enthusiasm. Clients should feel reassured and in capable hands; advisors should feel respected as professionals, not gamified.

## Anti-references

- Generic navy + sky-blue + orange-CTA "SaaS fintech" look (the current palette) — reads as a template, undermines the vetted-expertise premise.
- Warm cream/beige/paper AI-generated default — soft, indistinct, doesn't fit "exacting."
- Luxury private-banking cliché (black + gold, serif wordmarks, velvet-rope styling) — wrong register, this is a working tool, not a status symbol.
- Growth-hacky consumer SaaS patterns: countdown urgency, gradient hero text, confetti/gamification on serious financial/career decisions.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility over cleverness** — every screen should read as "this platform vets its advisors." No gimmicks, no filler stats.
2. **Booking is the critical path** — minimize friction from discovery to confirmed session on every surface that touches it (explore, profile, booking flow).
3. **Status at a glance** — advisors and admins need KYC/application/booking state scannable without reading; their practice depends on it.
4. **Calm under pressure** — clients often arrive stressed about a real decision; avoid manufactured urgency or aggressive CTAs.
5. **One consistent app shell** — client-facing and advisor/admin-facing surfaces share the same visual language so trust transfers across the whole product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, visible focus rings on all interactive elements, full keyboard navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for any motion added.
